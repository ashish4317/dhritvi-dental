Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a root canal treatment painful?

The treatment is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain during the procedure. Some mild soreness afterwards is common and usually settles within a few days. Your dentist will explain what to expect for your tooth.

How often should I get a dental checkup?

Most people are advised to visit every six months. If you have gum disease, braces or other ongoing conditions, your dentist may suggest more frequent visits.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes. If you have severe pain, swelling, bleeding or a broken or knocked-out tooth, call the clinic straight away using the emergency number on this page so we can see you as early as possible.

How long do whitening results last?

It depends on your habits. Tea, coffee, smoking and poor brushing can bring staining back sooner. Good home care and regular cleaning help the results last longer.

Are braces or aligners suitable for adults?

Yes, many adults have orthodontic treatment. After an examination, the dentist will tell you whether braces or clear aligners suit your teeth and how long treatment is likely to take.

How do I know what my treatment will cost?

Cost depends on the treatment you need. After your examination, the dentist will explain the plan and the estimated cost before any treatment begins.

How does online booking work?

Choose a service, date and time in the booking form and submit it. Our team will call you to confirm the appointment. Your request is not final until we confirm it.
