Dhritvi Advanced Dental Care ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

When you request an appointment, we collect the details you enter in the form:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • The service, date and time you prefer, and whether you are a new or returning patient
  • Any notes or symptoms you choose to share

If you submit a review, we display the name and comment you enter.

2. Why we collect it

  • To contact you and confirm, change or cancel your appointment
  • To prepare for your visit
  • To respond to your questions

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How it is stored and who can see it

Appointment requests are recorded in a Google Sheet and sent by email to clinic staff using Google services. Only authorised clinic staff have access. If you tap the WhatsApp button after booking, the details you send are shared with us through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy terms.

The page also loads fonts, icons and maps from third parties (such as Google). These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address.

4. How long we keep it

We keep appointment request details for [period, e.g. 12 months] unless you become a patient, in which case your records are kept as required for medical record keeping.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you submitted through this website by contacting us using the details below.

6. Medical information

Please do not send urgent medical information through the booking form. For emergencies, call the clinic directly.
